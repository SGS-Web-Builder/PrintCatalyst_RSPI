CAMPAIGN SETTINGS

Schedule ad

The default plays only when no scheduled ad is active, or when active media cannot play. Choosing a new default replaces the previous one.

Campaign period

Repeats are anchored to the start date and time. For example: start Monday at 14:00, repeat weekly, play for 60 minutes. Months and years use calendar dates, clamped to the last day when needed.

Videos play to their end (maximum 10 minutes per turn). Overlapping active campaigns rotate in library order. Changes reach the screen within about 5 seconds.
